Total: **547 source/data/reference assertions**, plus **21 GDScript files** parsed by the vendored grammar.

The combat math check compares 12,000 seeded sector cases against an independent angle-based oracle. All agree. The retained town geometry suite also compares 10,000 seeded segment/rectangle cases against Shapely. These are independent Python references, not Godot physics or state-machine runs.

Four intentional source mutations are rejected: restoring a homing strike, refunding weapon cooldown on cancel, disabling mossling detection, and scaling the warning footprint. See `combat_negative_controls.json`. Grammar negative controls reject deliberately malformed syntax.

Appearance, actor, wardrobe, navigation, world map, save-store, shared geometry and main scene files remain byte-identical to the supplied 0.2.1 base. Version labels, combat simulation/input/presentation, tests and documentation are updated. Packaging checks and exact hashes are in `BUILD_MANIFEST.json`.

## Native checks included for your PC

Run `Run Tests.bat`, select your installed Godot executable, and read `test-results.log`. The launcher checks both exit codes and reported errors and runs:

```text
godot --editor --headless --audio-driver Dummy --path . --quit
godot --headless --audio-driver Dummy --path . --script res://tests/test_runner.gd
godot --headless --audio-driver Dummy --path . --script res://tests/frontier_runner.gd
godot --headless --audio-driver Dummy --path . --script res://tests/appearance_runner.gd
godot --headless --audio-driver Dummy --path . --script res://tests/combat_runner.gd
godot --headless --audio-driver Dummy --path . --script res://tests/smoke_runner.gd
```

`combat_runner.gd` covers all enemy definitions, visibility-gated detection, stationary hits, lateral/behind/range dodges, re-entering before impact, locked facing and position, hit-then-move timing, movement-before-hit cancellation, cooldown spam rejection, no attack queue, wall/town entry protection and warning mesh orientation/material isolation. Existing suites retain progression, inventory, saving, appearance, fishing, town-route and manual-action checks. The scene suite also exercises force-move input and the warning node.

Tests disable automatic saving for simulated characters. Existing disk round-trips use `user://test_profiles/`, not the real three profile slots. Running the actual game or pressing Save updates the selected real profile normally.

## Manual combat acceptance — pending

Back up the profile folder, continue your existing character and bring cooked food. Approach a single mossling without attacking: it should notice you outside town and show an engagement cue without an instant hit. During its orange-sector windup, strike once, wait for your damage popup, then move sideways or behind it before impact. Check that the sector holds its direction, the enemy misses, and your health does not drop from that swing. Shift + left-click must force movement even over an enemy pick box.

Repeat by standing still inside the sector: damage should apply once. Move out and back in before the countdown ends: you should still be hittable. A new enemy windup may aim at your new position. Dodging one enemy grants no immunity against another. After impact, recovery should provide a readable opening before pursuit resumes.

Start a player swing and move before it lands: no damage should be awarded, and repeated attack clicks must not bypass the remaining weapon cooldown or cancel your escape path. Wait without clicking: readiness must not auto-start another attack. Recheck one-click mining, chopping, fishing and crafting.

Enter Hearthmere and Northreach Camp during a windup. Enemy damage must cancel, movement must remain outside the body-safe town boundaries, and return routes must keep their previous protection. Check obstacles, corners and the Watchwarden courtyard. A wall must block proximity detection and a strike at impact.

Finally, save/reload and verify colors, jeans/slacks selection, money, bank, equipment, both quests and levels. This update adds no save fields or schema changes. Check input/UI at your window size, including Shift-click, the cooldown bar and countdown readability.

## Remaining limits

This remains single-player and is not anti-macro protection. No roll, invulnerability frame system, stamina, attack combos, multiplayer validation or capsule/bone hitboxes are added. Strike collision tests the player ground-position point against an analytic sector; the 48-segment visual fan approximates its curved edge. Creature art/animation is still procedural prototype content. Runtime quality and balance need the native and manual checks above.
